General Garden Care Tips

💧

Watering

Water early morning or evening. Check soil 2cm deep — if moist, skip. Avoid waterlogging. Water at the base, not on leaves.

☀️

Sunlight

Most outdoor plants need 6-8 hours. Indoor plants prefer bright indirect light near windows. Rotate pots monthly.

🪱

Soil Health

Add organic compost every 3-4 months. Mix cocopeat + garden soil + compost (1:1:1) for pots. Well-draining soil prevents root rot.

✂️

Pruning

Prune to remove dead branches. Pinch tips for bushy growth. Sterilize tools before cutting to prevent disease spread.

🐛

Pest Control

Spray neem oil (5ml/L water) every 2 weeks. Check leaf undersides weekly. Isolate infected plants immediately.

🌱

Fertilizing

Use vermicompost or kitchen compost every 4-6 weeks in growing season. Banana peel water is great for potassium.
